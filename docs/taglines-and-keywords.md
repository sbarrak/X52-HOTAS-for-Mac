## One-paragraph descriptions

**GitHub "About" (350 characters or fewer)**

> Program the Saitek / Logitech X52 and X52 Pro on Apple Silicon Macs: modes, pinkie shift, macros, tap/hold, keystrokes,
> a programmable screen, live and alarm lights. X-Plane 12 plugin plus a menu bar app. Ready-made profiles for every
> X-Plane aircraft.

**Short blurb (for a store, forum or social post)**

> X52 Pro is a native Apple Silicon X-Plane 12 plugin and menu bar app that gives the Saitek / Logitech X52 and X52 Pro
> everything the Windows-only Logitech software did: three modes with a pinkie shift, macros, tap and hold, keystrokes,
> radio and autopilot pages on the throttle's screen, and lights that follow the simulator or flash when something is
> wrong. It comes with ready-made profiles for the X-Plane 12 aircraft and a manual. Free and open source (MIT).

X-Plane 12 plugin plus a menu bar app." --add-topic x-plane,x-plane-12,x-plane-plugin,flight-simulator,saitek,logitech,x52,x52-pro,hotas,joystick,macos,apple-silicon,arm64,menu-bar-app,swiftui,cpp
